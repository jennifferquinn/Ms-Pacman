Deep Q-Learning Concepts Simplified

Reinforcement Learning (RL) is a paradigm of Machine Learning where an **Agent** (Ms. Pac-Man) learns to make optimal decisions by interacting with an **Environment** (the maze), receiving **Rewards** (eating pellets/ghosts) or **Penalties** (getting caught).

1

State (S) & Action (A)

The State represents what Ms. Pac-Man sees: relative distances to nearest dots, power pellets, walls, and active ghosts. The Action is one of 4 directions: Up, Down, Left, or Right.

2

Q-Function & Neural Net

Q(s, a) estimates expected future total reward for taking action a in state s. Standard Q-tables grow too huge for complex visual games, so a Deep Neural Network is used as a function approximator.

3

Epsilon-Greedy Policy

Balancing Exploration (trying random moves to discover new rewards) and Exploitation (choosing the move with highest predicted Q-value). Epsilon (ε) starts high (1.0) and decays over time as the AI learns.

Experience Replay Buffer

In consecutive video frames, states are strongly correlated (state at step 100 looks identical to step 101). If trained sequentially, the neural network suffers from catastrophic forgetting. Experience Replay stores state transitions (State, Action, Reward, Next State, Done) in a memory buffer and updates the model using random mini-batches, stabilizing neural network convergence.
